Self-hosted bulletin to slide generator

Church Slides Studio

Paste a bulletin, upload backgrounds, and export JPG slides (default 1920x1080) with optional Ollama vision QA.

Quick Start

  1. Paste bulletin text or upload a document.
  2. Upload title & content backgrounds.
  3. Generate slides and download the ZIP.

Backgrounds

Title background
Content background

Slide Settings

Defaults to 1920x1080. Adjust layout boxes in data/settings.json if needed.

Ollama Settings

Settings save to data/settings.json and can be overridden by .env.
